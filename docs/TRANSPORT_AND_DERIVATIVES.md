# Transport properties and equilibrium derivatives

Two modules extend the local thermochemistry:

- `rocket_propulsion.thermochemistry.transport` — gas viscosity, frozen and
  reacting thermal conductivity, transport specific heats and Prandtl numbers
  in the formulation of NASA CEA (RP-1311 Part I, chapter 5), plus Sutherland
  and Chapman–Enskog fallbacks.
- `rocket_propulsion.thermochemistry.equilibrium_derivatives` — frozen and
  shifting-equilibrium `cp`, `cv`, `gamma`, `gamma_s = (dln p/dln rho)_s`,
  `(dln V/dln T)_p`, `(dln V/dln p)_T` and sound speed for an
  `EquilibriumResult` (RP-1311 Part I, sections 2.5–2.6), and a
  finite-difference check that re-solves the equilibrium with a provider.

Both are pure Python (standard library only). Every equation below was read in
the cited source during this work; every test value is either NASA CEA 3.3.4
output generated by `scripts/generate_cea_transport_points.py` or a value read
from a cited table.

## Data

`src/rocket_propulsion/thermochemistry/data/nasa_cea_transport.json` is
extracted by `scripts/sync_nasa_transport.py` from NASA's `data/trans.inp` at
commit `2f79a647e85737742fb08a127da00d1ea1d069da` (SHA-256
`379c514a…c3334d41`, Apache-2.0; 66 species, 41 binary interaction records,
379 coefficient lines). The fixed columns are those of CEA's own reader
(`source/database_compile.f90`, subroutine `utran`). `--check` re-extracts and
fails on any difference. The trans.inp file itself is not bundled; download it
from the pinned commit to re-run the script.

## Transport formulation (RP-1311 Part I, chapter 5)

| Quantity | Equation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| species `eta_i`, `lambda_i` | (5.1) `ln y = A ln T + B/T + C/T^2 + D` | fit units µP and µW/(cm K) (section 5.1) |
| mixture viscosity | (5.3) | `phi_ij` from (5.7) with trans.inp `eta_ij`, else (5.5) |
| frozen conductivity | (5.4), `psi_ij` (5.6) | |
| reaction conductivity | (5.8)–(5.13), `A* = 1.1` | reactions: independent basis of element-conserving reactions |
| `lambda_eq` | (5.2) | `lambda_fr + lambda_re` |
| transport `cp` | (5.14)–(5.18) | gas species used for transport only |
| Prandtl numbers | (5.19a,b) | |

Checked against the CEA source at the same commit
(`source/equilibrium.f90`, `compute_transport_properties`): the rigid-sphere
estimate of a missing `eta_ij` (constant `5.656854 = 4 sqrt 2`) is algebraically
equation (5.5) inserted in (5.7); the reaction sum uses `1.1` and a factor
`0.6 = 3/5`, i.e. equation (5.13); CEA's interval selection extrapolates
outside a fit's range.

Deliberate differences from CEA:

1. **Fail-closed range.** A species fit outside its temperature range raises
   `DomainError` (`transport_temperature_out_of_range`) unless
   `allow_extrapolation=True`, which reproduces CEA's selection with a warning.
   H, O, OH fits start at 1000 K, so cold exit states need the flag. An
   interaction fit outside its range is replaced by equation (5.5) with a
   warning (RP-1311 uses (5.5) whenever `eta_ij` is not available).
2. **Missing species.** CEA estimates viscosity and conductivity of species
   absent from trans.inp (HO2, H2O2, O3, HCO, COOH, …) with a correlation that
   RP-1311 Part I does not document. They are omitted here, with a warning;
   more than `maximum_omitted_mole_fraction` (default 1e-3) raises
   `transport_data_missing`. The CEA conductivity estimate is available as
   `modified_eucken_conductivity` for explicit use.
3. **Species set.** CEA selects transport species by cumulative abundance;
   here every species above `minimum_mole_fraction` (1e-10) is used.
The NASA-basis repair removes a former scale difference: both these
thermochemical modules and CEA 3.3.4 now use the model convention
`R = 8314.51 J/(kmol K)`. The reference fixtures are compared without the
former SI rescaling; their acceptance tolerances are retained. Generic
SI constants and reference-fluid model constants keep their own meanings.
See [NASA_THERMOCHEMICAL_BASIS_REVIEW.md](NASA_THERMOCHEMICAL_BASIS_REVIEW.md).

Fallbacks: `sutherland_viscosity` (U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 eq. (51),
`beta = 1.458e-6`, `S = 110.4 K`; the standard notes the law fails at very
high and low temperatures) and `chapman_enskog_viscosity` with
`neufeld_collision_integral` (Neufeld et al. 1972 constants as reproduced by
IDAES, citing Poling et al. eqs. 9-3.9/9-4.3; the 1972 paper was not opened and
no validity range of `T*` was verified).

## Derivative formulation (RP-1311 Part I, sections 2.5–2.6)

For gas-only mixtures the temperature derivatives solve (2.56) and (2.58),
the pressure derivatives (2.64) and (2.66); then `cp,e` is (2.59), the volume
derivatives (2.50)–(2.51), `cv` (2.70), `gamma` (2.72), `gamma_s` (2.73), the
sound speed `a = sqrt(n R T gamma_s)` (2.74) and `(dln p/dln T)_s` (2.75).
Frozen values keep composition fixed. `finite_difference_derivatives`
re-solves assigned-T-and-p problems with the provider and evaluates central
differences, and the sound speed from `a^2 = (dp/drho)_s` (2.67) with both
points on the isentrope.

## Verification against NASA CEA 3.3.4

`tests/reference_data/cea_transport_points.json` (CEA 3.3.4; `thermo.lib`
SHA-256 `8e5df1cc…3ec52a`, `trans.lib` `c203d933…cb834111`) holds 17
pure-species states, 4 fixed mixtures and the chamber and throat of the nine
LOX/LH2, LOX/CH4, LOX/RP-1 points of `cea_gate_points.json` (68.9476 bar,
infinite-area combustor). Largest measured relative deviations
(`tests/test_transport_and_derivatives.py`):

| Group | Quantity | Measured | Test tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| pure species | `eta`, `lambda` | 3.3e-16 | 1e-12 |
| fixed mixtures | `eta`, `lambda_fr` | 1.0e-8 | 1e-7 |
| fixed mixtures | `lambda_eq`, `Pr_fr`, `Pr_eq` | 5.7e-6 (R) | 2e-5 |
| LOX/LH2 | `eta` / `lambda_fr` / `lambda_eq` / `Pr_eq` | 4.9e-6 / 5.4e-5 / 3.8e-4 / 1.9e-4 | 1e-4 / 3e-4 / 1e-3 / 1e-3 |
| LOX/CH4 | same | 5.0e-5 / 1.1e-4 / 4.2e-4 / 2.5e-4 | same |
| LOX/RP-1 | same | 3.3e-5 / 7.0e-5 / 3.3e-4 / 2.8e-4 | same |
| CEA composition | `gamma_s` | 2.8e-8 | 1e-7 |
| CEA composition | `cp,e`, `cv,e` (R-rescaled) | 4.9e-8, 6.8e-8 | 2e-6 |
| CEA composition | `cp,fr`, `cv,fr` (R-rescaled) | 1.1e-6, 1.3e-6 | 2e-6 |
| CEA composition | sound speed (R-rescaled; raw 2.9e-6) | 1.5e-8 | 2e-6 |
| local provider at CEA T, p | `gamma_s`, sound speed, `cp,e` | 2.7e-8, 9.4e-9, 4.4e-8 | 1e-6, 1e-5, 1e-5 |

The rocket-state transport deviations track the omitted mole fraction (at most
3.3e-4 for these states). The frozen `cp` residual of 1.1e-6 comes from CEA
trace species (CH2OH, CH3O, …) absent from the bundled thermodynamic catalog.

Independent checks:

- finite differences with the local provider (step 1e-3) agree with the
  linearized derivatives within 3.8e-6 (`cp,e`), 1.6e-6
  (`(dln V/dln T)_p`), 7.9e-9 (`(dln V/dln p)_T`) and 1.1e-8 (sound speed);
  tolerance 2e-5 covers O(step^2) truncation and the solver's 2e-9
  composition tolerance;
- frozen limit: pure N2 gives `gamma_s = cp/cv`, `(dln V/dln T)_p = 1`,
  `(dln V/dln p)_T = -1`, and matches CEA's `gamma_s` within 1e-7; Bridgman
  form (2.68) equals `-gamma_s` to 1e-12;
- Sutherland: 1.78938e-5 Pa s at 288.15 K against 1.7894e-5 in U.S.
  Standard Atmosphere 1976 Table III (tolerance 3e-5, the table's rounding);
- Chapman–Enskog with Svehla's N2 constants (sigma 3.798 Å, eps/k 71.4 K)
  against Svehla's NASA TR R-132 Table III: -0.04 %, -0.41 %, -0.56 %,
  -0.78 % at 100, 300, 1000, 2000 K. Svehla used tabulated collision integrals;
  the gap is the Neufeld fit's, so the tolerance is 1 %.

## Not verified / not included

- CEA's correlation for species missing from trans.inp and its
  abundance-based species selection (read in the source, not reproduced).
- Ionized species (CEA's electron and ion estimates), condensed phases
  (RP-1311 excludes them from transport), and the triple-phase `gamma_s` of
  RP-1311 section 3.5.
- The validity range of the Neufeld fit and the original 1972 paper.
- Transport states below 1000 K for mixtures containing H, O or OH unless
  extrapolation is requested.
